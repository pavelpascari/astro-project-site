The template aims at WCAG 2.2 AA on every page it renders: the landing page, docs, gallery and blog.

## What you get without doing anything

- **Keyboard:** a "Skip to content" link, visible focus rings, `/` to filter a long docs sidebar, menus that close
  on Escape and give focus back, and live-example editors you can leave with Escape then Tab.
- **Screen readers:** one `<h1>` and a `<main>` per page; every landmark, button and link has a name (copy buttons
  included, not just a tooltip); status messages ("Copied", "3 pages match", the gallery count) are announced;
  external gallery links say "(external site)"; decorative icons are hidden.
- **Low vision:** text, links, buttons and focus rings meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both themes; with
  "Increase contrast" turned on in the system, secondary text goes to 7:1. Zooming is never blocked, and pages
  reflow down to 320 px wide without scrolling sideways.
- **Touch:** targets are at least 44×44 px on touch screens.
- **Language:** `<html lang>` and `dir` on every page, and the template's words in the site's language (see
  [Languages](/docs/recipes/i18n/)).

## What is yours to look after

Your content: alt text for images in posts and gallery posters (`posterAlt`), headings in order, link text that
says where it goes, and captions for video. A custom `theme.accent` is checked for contrast at build time (see
[Theming](/docs/guides/theming/)); colours you set in `customCss` are not.

## How it is checked

On every change, CI:

1. builds the template's own site and the starter, then runs html-validate's accessibility rules and checks of
   its own (lang, one `<h1>`, a `<main>`) on every page: `npm run test:a11y`;
2. builds a small site in French and in Arabic (right to left) and runs the same checks, plus the language ones:
   `npm run test:i18n`;
3. checks the theme's colour pairs against WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes, and that no component has English text
   written into it (`npm test`).

A handful of findings in Starlight's own markup (its two tables of contents share a name, as only one is shown
at a time) are listed, with the reason, in `scripts/check-html.mjs`. Layout at different screen sizes and in
three browsers is checked by the UX harness described in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
